# Ember Knight

A hand-built pixel platformer. Eight phases, two bosses, one flame.

## v1.0.0 — the first version

**Campaign**
- Eight phases from the Ember Road to the Ember Heart, with per-phase lore
- The Ash Warden (melee bruiser) and the Ash King (a mage who floats, hexes and
  summons casters; damage only lands while he is stunned)
- Ember dash and Ember Lantern abilities, light-only bridges, the rising tide,
  crumbling platforms, a coin ledger and phase select

**The fight**
- The King scales with his phase: rain 3/5/7 drops, eruptions 3/4/6, snares
  3/4/5, twin bolts 2/3/4, meteors 1/2/3, chained casts, and two piercing
  hexer summons from phase 2

**Presentation**
- Pixel knight with six colour schemes, a framed 960x540 presentation with
  status strips, and a title-screen diorama
- Recorded boss themes, intro voices, attack and death stings, and the
  King's phase stings
- A story prologue, an ending that earns the title "The Ember Knight", and a
  credits screen

**Desktop**
- Portable Windows build (`Ember-Knight-1.0.0.exe`), windowed 1440x900,
  F11 fullscreen, saves kept in `%APPDATA%\Ember Knight`
